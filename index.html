My Salah

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

My Salah keeps your information on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and we never see, sell or share your personal information.

Who we are

My Salah is a prayer times app published by Gen1 Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Information the app uses

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Your location, or a city you chooseTo calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and distances to your mosquesOn your phone
Name, age, phone number, email address and gender, if you enter themTo personalise the app. Gender decides whether the optional prayer break feature is shownOn your phone
Prayer break dates, if you use this featureTo pause prayer reminders on the dates you chooseOn your phone
Mosques, iqamah times, reminders, tasbih counts and settingsTo run the app's featuresOn your phone

Phone number and email address are optional. The app works fully without them.

Location

If you allow location access, the app reads your position while you are using it. It does not track you in the background.

To show the name of your city, the app uses Android's built-in geocoding service, which sends your coordinates to Google over an encrypted connection and returns a place name. This happens only when you choose to use your location, and the result is kept only on your phone. Google's handling of that request is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can choose a city from a list instead, and the app will not use your location at all.

Prayer break

The prayer break feature lets women privately pause prayer reminders on the days they choose. The dates you enter stay on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are only used to decide when reminders are sent.

Notifications and sounds

Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them. If you choose your own audio file as a reminder sound, the app copies it into your phone's notification sounds folder so Android can play it.

Home screen widgets

Widgets read the same information the app keeps on your phone. They do not send anything anywhere.

Backups

If you use Back up now, the app creates a file with your settings and mosques and opens your phone's share options. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as Google Drive, WhatsApp or email. We never receive it. Keep it somewhere private, since it contains the information you entered in the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't run servers or accounts, so we have no copy of your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information.

Children

My Salah is designed for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app sends no personal information to us.

Your choices

  • You can edit or clear your details in the app under Settings, Your details.
  • You can turn location access on or off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • You can end or clear a prayer break at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Security

Your information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. The only information that leaves your phone, the location lookup described above, is sent over an encrypted connection.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
